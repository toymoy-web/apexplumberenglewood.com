What to do before you call in an emergency

These steps protect people and property while help is on the way.

  • Close the main water shutoff if a leak will not stop at a fixture valve.
  • Stop using all drains if sewage is backing up anywhere in the house.
  • Keep clear of standing water near outlets and appliances.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call Xcel Energy or 911 from outside.
  • Take photos of any damage for your insurance claim.

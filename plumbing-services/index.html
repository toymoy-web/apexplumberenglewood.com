Emergency and leak repairs

When water is going where it should not, start with burst pipe repair for a split or separated line, or frozen pipe repair when a cold snap has stopped the water entirely. Emergency calls in Englewood are answered 24/7.

Hidden leaks are found with leak detection, which uses meters, acoustic equipment and thermal imaging before anything is opened. Leaks under concrete floors are covered by slab leak detection and repair.

Water heaters and heating

For tank heaters, water heater repair covers pilots, elements, valves and leaks, and installing or replacing a water heater covers new tank, tankless and heat pump units with permits handled.

On-demand heaters get their own page, tankless water heater service, because descaling and error codes are a different job. Older Englewood homes heated by radiators are covered under boiler repair.

Drains and sewer lines

Slow and blocked drains start with drain cleaning. For grease or roots that return again and again, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall all the way around.

Damaged sewer laterals are repaired through sewer line repair, including spot repairs and lining. When the whole line has failed, sewer line replacement compares open trench and trenchless options.

Water supply lines and water quality

Old galvanized pipe inside the house is replaced through repiping. The buried line from the curb stop to the house is covered by water line repair and replacement, which also explains Englewood's lead service line program.

For water quality, water filtration installation covers certified filters for lead and taste, and water softener installation addresses hardness and scale.

Fixtures, gas and appliances

Everyday fixture work includes toilet repair and installation, faucet and sink repair, garbage disposal repair and shower and bathtub installation.

Natural gas piping for ranges, dryers, grills and tankless heaters is handled through gas line installation and repair. Basements that take on groundwater need working pumps, covered by sump pump installation and repair.

Emergency calls and scheduled work

Some problems cannot wait. Water you cannot shut off at a fixture, sewage coming up a drain, a leaking water heater tank, a boiler failing in freezing weather and a sump pump that has stopped during a storm are all handled 24/7.

Everything else is usually better scheduled. A dripping faucet, a slow sink, a water heater that is getting old, or a repipe you are planning all cost less on a weekday visit, and non-emergency estimates are free. When you call, we tell you honestly which category your problem is in.

Every job is priced before work starts. The factors that move the number are the same across services: the time of the call, how easy the pipe is to reach, the age and material of the plumbing, and whether the City of Englewood requires a permit for the work. All work follows the Colorado Plumbing Code, which is built on the 2021 IPC (International Plumbing Code).

What to do first in any plumbing emergency

Whatever the service, these first steps keep people safe and limit damage.

  • Close the main water shutoff if water is leaking and a fixture valve will not stop it.
  • Keep away from standing water near outlets and appliances, and switch off power at the panel if safe.
  • Stop using all drains if sewage is backing up anywhere in the house.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call Xcel Energy or 911 from outside.
  • Photograph any damage before cleanup for your insurer.

Commercial, backflow and water damage

Businesses and apartment buildings are served through commercial plumbing, and annual certification required by Englewood Utilities is handled through backflow testing and prevention.

After a leak or flood, water mitigation covers emergency extraction and drying, and water damage restoration covers the full recovery. Both use a dedicated restoration phone line.

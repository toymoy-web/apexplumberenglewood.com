Frozen Pipe Repair in Englewood, CO

Frozen pipe repair means safely thawing a water line that has frozen solid, then checking it and repairing any split before water pressure returns. In Englewood, CO it is a winter emergency. A frozen line that thaws on its own can spray water from a crack you did not know was there, so it pays to act while the pipe is still frozen.

If a pipe is frozen now

  • Leave the faucet open.
  • Close the main if you suspect a split.
  • Warm the room, not the pipe with a flame.
  • Open cabinet doors on outside walls.

Safe steps while you wait

Most frozen-pipe damage happens during the thaw, not the freeze. These steps limit it.

  • Keep the affected faucet open so meltwater and pressure have somewhere to go.
  • If you see frost, bulging or a crack on the pipe, close the main shutoff before it thaws.
  • Turn the thermostat up and prop open the sink cabinets that sit against outside walls.
  • Warm a reachable pipe gently with a hair dryer or space heater kept well away from anything flammable.
  • Never use a torch, open flame or charcoal heater. They cause fires and can burst the pipe.

Englewood cold, and where it finds pipes

Front Range cold can arrive in hours. On December 21, 2022, the National Weather Service recorded the temperature at Denver International Airport falling 37 degrees in one hour, and the low the next morning reached 24 below zero. A house that is fine on a normal winter night can freeze pipes in a snap like that.

The IPC (International Plumbing Code) does not allow water pipe in exterior walls, attics or crawlspaces where it can freeze unless it is protected, but many older houses were plumbed long before current rules.

In Englewood's post-war bungalows, the usual trouble spots are kitchen supply lines in exterior walls, lines running through an unheated attached garage, crawlspaces under additions, and outdoor hose bibs. Houses heated by an older boiler can also freeze pipes if the boiler fails overnight, which our boiler repair page covers.

How a frozen pipe is thawed and repaired

  1. Find the frozen section. We trace from the fixture that has no water back to the cold spot, often an exterior wall or garage run.
  2. Protect the house. If a split is likely, the main stays off during thawing so water cannot escape.
  3. Thaw safely. The section is thawed with controlled heat, such as a pipe thawing machine or targeted warm air, never an open flame.
  4. Check for damage. With the line thawed, the pipe is inspected and pressure tested for splits.
  5. Repair and prevent. Any split section is replaced, and we suggest insulation, rerouting or heat tape where it will actually help.

If a pipe has already split and water is spraying, the job becomes a burst pipe repair, and the first step is closing the main.

What affects the cost of frozen pipe repair

Frozen pipe calls cluster on the coldest nights, and the final cost depends on what the thaw reveals.

Access to the frozen line

A line in an open basement or garage is quick to reach. One inside a finished wall may need an opening.

Split or no split

A line that thaws without damage costs far less than one that needs a section replaced.

Number of frozen runs

A deep cold snap can freeze several lines in the same house.

Timing

Most calls come overnight or early morning during a cold snap, and after-hours rates apply.

Prevention work

Insulation, frost-free hose bibs and rerouting lines away from cold walls cost less than the next repair.

Damage from a pipe that froze and burst is covered by many policies, provided the home was reasonably heated. Damage in a home left unheated is often excluded, so keep the heat on when you travel.

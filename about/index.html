How the service works

Englewood Apex Plumbing is a plumbing referral and dispatch service. The phone number on this site connects you with an independent, licensed plumbing provider who serves Englewood and nearby towns. That provider sets the price, performs the work and stands behind the warranty. We may receive compensation for those referrals, which is also stated in the footer of every page.

We built this site because plumbing emergencies are stressful and most people do not know which plumber to call at 2 a.m. The pages here are meant to help you understand what is probably wrong, what to do in the first few minutes, and what a fair repair looks like, whether or not you call us.

Every page on this site was written for Englewood specifically. The lead service line program, the sanitation district rules, the Allen Water Treatment Plant, the age of the housing and the Colorado cold all shape the advice.

What you can expect when you call

These are the standards every call is held to.

  • Your call is answered 24 hours a day, including weekends and holidays.
  • You are asked what you see, not pressured to book, and told plainly whether it is an emergency.
  • You get an estimated arrival window and are told if it changes.
  • The cause is diagnosed and a price is given before any repair begins.
  • Plumbing work in Colorado is done by plumbers licensed through the state, and you can ask for the license number at the door.

Have a question before you book?

Call and ask. You are under no obligation until you approve a price.

What we will not do

We will not tell you a repair is an emergency when it can wait until morning. We will not quote a price without seeing the problem, and we will not publish fake reviews, invented awards or claims about years in business on this site.

We also will not recommend replacing something that can reasonably be repaired. Many pages on this site say directly when a repair is the better value and when it is not, because that is the advice we would want ourselves.

After the repair

Work is done to the Colorado Plumbing Code, which is built on the 2021 IPC (International Plumbing Code), and permitted work such as water heater replacements, gas lines, repipes and sewer replacements goes through the city's permit and inspection process. The provider arranges that inspection as part of the job.

You receive an itemized invoice describing what was found and what was repaired, and for camera and leak detection work, the findings. Keep it with your home records, since insurers, buyers and home inspectors often ask for plumbing history.

Warranty terms come from the provider who did the work and are explained before you approve the job. If something does not look right after a repair, call the same number and it will be routed back to that provider.

Where we work

The line covers both of Englewood's main ZIP codes, 80110 and 80113, and the neighboring towns listed on our service areas page: Sheridan, Cherry Hills Village, Greenwood Village, western and central Centennial, and Denver's University Park.

The full list of plumbing work is on our plumbing services page. For flood and leak cleanup, our water damage restoration page has its own dedicated phone line.

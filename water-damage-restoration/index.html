Water Damage Restoration in Englewood, CO

Water damage restoration is the full process of recovering a home or business after water gets where it should not. It runs from water removal and drying through mold prevention and the repair of drywall, flooring and cabinets. In Englewood, CO restoration calls usually follow a burst pipe, a failed water heater, a sewer backup or a flooded basement, and the line for restoration is answered 24/7.

What to do in the first hour

The first steps protect your safety and your insurance claim.

  • Stop the source of water if you can, such as closing the main shutoff for a burst pipe.
  • Do not enter standing water where it may reach outlets or appliances. Switch off power at the panel if it is safe to reach.
  • Take photos and video of the water, damage and belongings before moving anything.
  • Move valuables, electronics and documents to a dry area.
  • Call your insurance company to start the claim, and keep a record of who you spoke to.

Not all water is the same

The restoration industry standard, IICRC S500, sorts water damage by how contaminated the water is. That decides what can be dried and what must be removed.

CategoryTypical sourceWhat it means
Category 1, clean waterBurst supply pipe or overflowing sink.Materials can often be dried in place if handled quickly.
Category 2, gray waterWashing machine or dishwasher overflow.Some porous materials may need removal.
Category 3, black waterSewer backup or outside floodwater.Porous materials are usually removed and the area disinfected.

Clean water becomes more contaminated the longer it sits, so a quick response can keep a Category 1 loss from becoming a bigger job.

How restoration works

Restoration starts with mitigation, stopping the damage from spreading. Standing water is extracted, wet materials that cannot be saved are removed, and air movers and dehumidifiers are set up. Our water mitigation page covers that first phase in detail.

Drying is measured, not guessed. Moisture meters and thermal cameras map how far water has spread into walls, floors and cabinets, and readings are taken daily until materials reach normal dry levels.

Once everything is dry, the rebuild begins. Drywall is replaced, flooring and baseboards reinstalled, and cabinets repaired or replaced. The goal is to return the space to how it was before the loss.

Englewood water damage: common sources

Englewood's many post-war basement homes are where most local water damage shows up. Burst pipes during cold snaps, failed water heaters, sump pumps that stop during a storm and sewer backups all end up in the basement first.

Cold snaps are a frequent trigger. On December 22, 2022, the temperature at Denver International Airport dropped to 24 below zero, and burst pipes follow that kind of cold across the metro. The plumbing repair itself is a separate job, covered on our burst pipe repair page.

Colorado's dry air helps materials dry once equipment is running, but it does not dry a soaked wall cavity or subfloor on its own. Hidden moisture is where mold starts.

What affects the cost of water damage restoration

Restoration is priced by the scope of damage, which is documented as the work progresses.

Amount of water and area affected

A small area of wet carpet is very different from a flooded finished basement.

Category of water

Sewage and floodwater require removal and disinfection of more materials.

Time before drying starts

Faster response usually means less material to remove and replace.

Materials involved

Hardwood, cabinets and plaster take more work than carpet and drywall.

Rebuild scope

Replacing flooring, drywall and cabinets is often the largest part of the cost.

Many homeowners policies pay for sudden, accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe. Flooding from outside and sewer backups are often excluded unless extra coverage was purchased. Documentation of moisture readings and damage helps the claim.

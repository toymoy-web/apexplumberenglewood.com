Where Duncan Park sits in Englewood

Duncan Park is a neighborhood name on the city's map and the name of a city park at 4880 S. Pennsylvania Street. The park has a pavilion, picnic area, playground, restroom, basketball court, horseshoe pits, a multi-use field and an off-leash dog area. Parking is on the street only.

We do not state exact neighborhood boundaries. The city published them on its map, so use that map to confirm whether your address is inside.

The City of Englewood adopted its Neighborhood Map on June 20, 2022, dividing the city into 40 neighborhoods. The city's Historic Preservation Commission chose the names and boundaries, combining historic subdivision names with modern day locations of significance. The names do not replace subdivision names on legal documents such as deeds.

Hot water, scale and Englewood's water supply

Englewood Utilities treats the city's drinking water at the Allen Water Treatment Plant, first built in 1958, using water from City Ditch and the South Platte River. The city has an active program aimed at improving taste, odor and hardness.

Published hardness figures vary by source, so we do not quote one. The reliable approach is to test your own tap with a kit and read Englewood Utilities' annual water quality report. Hardness is what builds scale in tank heaters and inside the heat exchanger of a tankless unit.

A tank that leaks from its body cannot be repaired and needs replacing. A heater that is merely slow to heat, or a tankless unit showing an error, may only need service. A technician can tell the difference once they see it.

Where Jason Park sits in Englewood

Jason Park is a neighborhood name on the city's map and the name of a city park at 4299 S. Jason Street. The park was renovated in 2023, adding a new pavilion and restroom, and it has a playground, basketball court, multi-use field and an off-leash dog area.

We do not state exact neighborhood boundaries here. The city published them on its map, so use that map to see where your address falls.

The City of Englewood adopted its Neighborhood Map on June 20, 2022, dividing the city into 40 neighborhoods. The city's Historic Preservation Commission chose the names and boundaries, combining historic subdivision names with modern day locations of significance. The names do not replace subdivision names on legal documents such as deeds.

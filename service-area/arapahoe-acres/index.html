Where Arapahoe Acres sits in Englewood

Arapahoe Acres is listed in the National Register summary as lying between East Bates and East Dartmouth Avenues and between South Marion and South Franklin Streets. It covers roughly 30 acres. The City of Englewood includes the name on the neighborhood map it adopted in 2022.

The district was built from 1949 to 1957. Edward Hawkins was the developer, overall designer and builder, and he designed most of the houses. Eugene Sternberg and Joseph Dion also designed some of them.

The City of Englewood adopted its Neighborhood Map on June 20, 2022, dividing the city into 40 neighborhoods. The city's Historic Preservation Commission chose the names and boundaries, combining historic subdivision names with modern day locations of significance. The names do not replace subdivision names on legal documents such as deeds.

Plumbing in a historic mid-century neighborhood

The homes here were built within a short span, so it is tempting to assume they share the same pipes. We do not make that assumption. Houses from the late 1940s and 1950s can contain a mix of materials, and repairs over the decades may have changed what is behind a wall.

The reliable way to find out is to look. A camera inspection shows the condition of a sewer line. Non-invasive leak detection can locate a leak without opening finished walls, which matters in homes where original finishes are part of the appeal.

If you are planning a remodel or any work that changes how the house looks from the street, ask the City of Englewood and History Colorado whether any review applies before work starts. Fixing a failed pipe is ordinary maintenance, but we cannot speak for the city on a larger project.

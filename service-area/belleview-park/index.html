Water bills, hidden leaks and winter care

A high water bill is the most common reason people look for a hidden leak. Englewood Utilities has been replacing water meters, so if you think the meter itself is wrong, contact Englewood Utilities billing and ask about having it checked. If the meter is right, a leak is the next suspect.

A simple test is to turn off every fixture and watch the meter dial or leak indicator. If it keeps moving, water is going somewhere. A running toilet is the most common cause, and a leak in the line to the house is the most costly.

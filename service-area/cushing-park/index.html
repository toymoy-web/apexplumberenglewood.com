Where Cushing Park sits in Englewood

Cushing Park is both a neighborhood name on the city's map and the name of a city park at 700 W. Dartmouth Avenue. The park has two pavilions, a playground, a basketball court, a skate park, a fitness court and a pond.

We do not claim exact neighborhood boundaries here. The city published them on its map, and they can differ from the subdivision names on a deed. To see where your address falls, use the city's neighborhood map.

The City of Englewood adopted its Neighborhood Map on June 20, 2022, dividing the city into 40 neighborhoods. The city's Historic Preservation Commission chose the names and boundaries, combining historic subdivision names with modern day locations of significance. The names do not replace subdivision names on legal documents such as deeds.

Sewer lines and who owns what

Englewood homes are served by several sanitation districts. South Platte Renew keeps an online map showing which district covers an address, and that is the right first step for any sewer question.

South Englewood Sanitation District No. 1 states the rule many owners follow. The sewer line from the building out to and including the tap into the district main belongs to the owner. Other districts may differ, so confirm yours.

In practice, a backup that keeps returning after the line has been cleared is usually a private repair. A camera shows whether roots are entering a joint, whether a section has sagged or whether the pipe has cracked. That decides between cleaning, a spot repair and replacement.

Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

Four quick steps for any Greenwood Village property.

Shut off the main valve

In homes, close the main where the line enters. In offices, building staff usually know the valve for each restroom group or floor.

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off circuits near the leak at the panel, and in commercial kitchens turn off dish machines on the affected line.

Clear the immediate area

Keep customers, staff or family away from wet floors and move equipment or furniture out of the path of the water.

Two kinds of Greenwood Village plumbing

Greenwood Village was incorporated in 1950 and now covers about 8.3 square miles, with around 15,700 residents at the 2020 census. Its western neighborhoods are mostly single-family homes on generous lots, while the eastern side takes in a large share of the Denver Tech Center.

Residential calls look much like Cherry Hills Village: long service lines, mature landscaping, irrigation systems, and larger homes with more fixtures and water heaters. Commercial calls are about restrooms, kitchen drains, water heaters and backflow devices in offices, hotels and restaurants.

Restaurants and hotel kitchens also fall under the IPC (International Plumbing Code) requirement for grease interceptors where grease is produced. Commercial properties follow their water provider's cross-connection rules for annual backflow testing, and our commercial plumbing service handles testing, repairs and after-hours work.

Around-the-clock emergency dispatch covers both the west-side homes and the offices, hotels and restaurants in the Tech Center.

Burst Pipe Repair in Englewood, CO

Burst pipe repair is the emergency repair of a water line that has split, cracked or separated at a joint and is releasing water under pressure. In Englewood, CO bursts spike during hard freezes, but they also happen when corroded pipe or a failed fitting finally gives out. The line is answered 24/7, and the first step is always stopping the water.

What to do in the first five minutes

The damage from a burst pipe grows every minute water is running. These steps are safe for anyone.

  • Close the main shutoff. In most Englewood basement houses it is on the front foundation wall where the line comes in, often next to an indoor meter.
  • If you cannot find or turn the main, the curb stop at the property line can shut off the house. It usually needs a long curb key.
  • Switch off electricity to any room where water is near outlets, fixtures or appliances, if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain water still in the pipes.
  • Move valuables and electronics out of the path of the water, then take photos.

Why pipes burst in Englewood

Freezing is the best known cause. Water expands as it freezes, and the pressure builds between the ice blockage and the closed faucet until the pipe splits. On December 21, 2022, the National Weather Service recorded a 37-degree temperature drop in a single hour at Denver International Airport, and the next morning's low was 24 below zero. Cold like that finds pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages quickly.

Age is the other common cause. Englewood's median house was built around 1955, and galvanized pipe from that era rusts from the inside until a threaded joint or thin section fails. That kind of burst can happen in July.

High water pressure adds stress to any pipe. The IPC (International Plumbing Code) calls for a pressure reducing valve where street pressure exceeds 80 psi, and many homes have one at the main. When it fails, pressure in the house can climb and push a weak fitting or supply hose past its limit.

Plumbing repair and water damage cleanup are two jobs

What affects the cost of burst pipe repair

Emergency repairs are priced before work begins, once the plumber has seen the break.

Location of the break

An exposed basement pipe is quick to reach. One inside a finished wall or ceiling needs an opening.

Pipe material

Copper and PEX repairs are clean. Old galvanized pipe may crumble near the break and need a longer section.

Number of breaks

A deep freeze can split a line in more than one place, and each is found and fixed.

Time of the call

Most bursts happen at inconvenient times, and after-hours rates apply.

Follow-up work

Insulation, rerouting or a new pressure reducing valve can prevent a repeat.

Many homeowners policies pay for sudden, accidental damage when a pipe bursts, often including the cost of opening a wall to reach it. The pipe repair itself and damage from long-term leaks or an unheated house may not be. Photograph everything and call your insurer early.

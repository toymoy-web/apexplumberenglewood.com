Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Overview

This policy explains what information Englewood Apex Plumbing ("we") collects through apexplumberenglewood.com and the phone numbers shown on it, and how that information is used. This website has no contact forms, accounts or online payments.

Phone calls

Calls to the phone numbers on this site are routed through a call tracking service. Call details, such as the caller's number, time, duration and general location, may be logged, and calls may be recorded for quality, dispute resolution and billing purposes.

To connect you with a plumber or restoration provider, the information you share on the call, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem, is passed to the independent service provider who handles your job.

Website visits

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays maps using an embedded Google Map. Those services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under Google's privacy policy. We do not sell personal information collected through this site.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to respond and, where relevant, to arrange service.

How long information is kept

Call records and emails are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, including billing, quality review and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it, subject to legal and billing retention requirements, by emailing contact@apexplumberenglewood.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

This policy may be revised. The date below shows when it was last updated.

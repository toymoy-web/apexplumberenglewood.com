Toilet Repair & Installation in Englewood, CO

Toilet repair fixes the parts that make a toilet run, leak, rock or clog, such as the flapper, fill valve, flush valve, wax ring and closet bolts. Toilet installation replaces the whole fixture. In Englewood, CO many homes still have toilets and flanges from the 1950s and 1960s, and a worn flange under an old toilet is often the real reason it keeps leaking.

What to do when a toilet is overflowing

A few quick steps limit the mess and protect the ceiling below an upstairs bathroom.

  • Turn the supply valve behind the toilet clockwise until the water stops.
  • If the valve will not turn, lift the tank lid and raise the float to stop the fill, then close the main shutoff.
  • Do not flush again to test it until the clog is cleared.
  • If other drains in the house are also backing up, stop using all water. The clog is in the main line, not the toilet.
  • Mop up quickly around the base so water does not seep under flooring or into the ceiling below.

When several fixtures back up together, the problem needs main line drain cleaning, not a toilet auger.

Old flanges, old toilets and Englewood water

In a house built around 1955, the toilet flange often sits on a cast iron closet bend, and the flange itself may be cast iron or an early metal ring. Over decades the bolt slots rust or crack. A new wax ring on a broken flange will not stay sealed, which is why a toilet that leaks at the base keeps leaking after a DIY reset.

Englewood's city water carries enough minerals that the utility is working on hardness. Scale builds up in the rim jets and siphon jet of the bowl and slowly weakens the flush. Cleaning the jets can restore a flush, but a very old toilet with a small trapway is often worth replacing.

Choosing a replacement toilet

Since 2016, Colorado law has required new toilets sold in the state to carry the EPA WaterSense label, which means 1.28 gallons per flush or less. Modern WaterSense toilets are engineered to flush well at that volume, unlike some early low-flow models from the 1990s.

The details that matter at install are the rough-in, usually 12 inches from the wall to the flange center in older Englewood homes, the bowl shape, and the seat height. Comfort-height toilets suit many adults. A new supply line and shutoff valve are worth replacing at the same time, since old valves often fail when they are finally turned.

What affects the cost of toilet work

Toilet repairs are usually quick, but a few factors change the price.

The part

A flapper or fill valve is a low-cost part. Flange repair takes more time and materials.

Flange condition

A cracked cast iron flange may need a repair ring or a new flange before the toilet can be reset.

New toilet choice

Standard, comfort-height and one-piece toilets vary in price, and you can supply your own.

Shutoff valve

An old valve that will not close is replaced, which adds a small cost.

After-hours timing

An overflowing toilet at night is handled 24/7, and after-hours rates apply.

Toilet repair is maintenance. Sudden water damage from an overflow or a failed supply line may be covered by your homeowners policy, while a slow leak at the base usually is not.

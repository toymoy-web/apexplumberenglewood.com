Boiler Repair in Englewood, CO

Boiler repair covers hot-water heating systems that warm a house through radiators or baseboard instead of ducts. The work includes restoring lost pressure, replacing leaking relief valves and failed circulator pumps, bleeding air, and fixing ignition faults on the burner. In Englewood, CO these systems are most common in houses built before 1950, and they tend to fail on the coldest night of the year.

Englewood's radiator houses

Census estimates compiled by Point2 put about 11 percent of Englewood's homes before 1940 and roughly another 9 percent in the 1940s. Many houses from that period were heated with a boiler and cast iron radiators, and a good number still are, either with the original system or with a newer boiler feeding the old radiators.

Hot-water heat is quiet, even and durable, but it depends on a few small parts working together. A pressure reducing fill valve keeps the system full, an expansion tank absorbs the water's growth as it heats, a circulator pump moves water through the loop, and a relief valve protects the boiler. When one of those parts fails, the symptoms show up in the radiators first.

Common boiler problems and what usually causes them

These are the faults we most often trace on residential hot-water boilers. The gauge on the front of the boiler shows pressure and temperature, and a cold system on a typical two-story house usually reads somewhere around 12 to 15 psi.

When no heat becomes a plumbing emergency

Colorado cold can arrive fast. On December 21, 2022, the National Weather Service recorded the temperature at Denver International Airport falling 37 degrees in a single hour as an Arctic front came through, and the low the next morning was 24 below zero.

In a house heated by a boiler, the first pipes at risk are the ones in exterior walls and unheated crawlspaces, including the boiler's own supply and return lines. If the heat is out during a hard freeze, open cabinet doors on outside walls and let a faucet drip while you wait. If a pipe has already frozen or split, our frozen pipe repair service handles thawing and repair safely.

Safe checks before you call

These take a minute and occasionally solve the problem. Stop at any step that feels unsafe.

  • Confirm the thermostat is set to heat and above room temperature, and replace its batteries if it has them.
  • Check that the boiler's service switch, usually on the wall or the side of the unit, is on.
  • Look at the pressure gauge and note the number, even if you are not sure what it means.
  • Look for water under the boiler, at the relief valve outlet, and under radiators.
  • If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds or you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or Xcel Energy from outside.

Please do not add water to a hot, empty boiler or try to force a relief valve closed. Both can cause damage or injury.

Repair or replace an older boiler

Old cast iron boilers can run for decades, so age alone is not a reason to replace one. Pumps, valves, expansion tanks and ignition parts are all replaceable, and on a sound boiler those repairs are usually the better value.

Replacement starts to make sense when the heat exchanger itself is cracked or leaking, when parts for the burner controls are no longer made, or when a high-efficiency condensing boiler would cut gas use enough to matter.

A new boiler also brings new venting and a gas supply sized to its input. In Colorado that work follows the state's Plumbing and Fuel Gas Codes, which are built on the 2021 IPC and the International Fuel Gas Code (IFGC), and the piping side falls under our gas line installation and repair work. We tell you which situation you are in rather than defaulting to a new unit.

What affects the cost of boiler repair in Englewood

Boiler calls are quoted after diagnosis, because the same cold-radiator symptom can come from a two-dollar air vent or a failed pump.

The failed part

Air vents and relief valves are inexpensive. Circulator pumps, controls and expansion tanks cost more.

Finding a leak

A leak in an exposed pipe is quick. One inside a wall or under a floor takes longer to locate and reach.

Age and brand

Parts for very old burners may need to be sourced or substituted, which can add time.

Time of the call

No-heat calls on a cold night are common, and after-hours rates apply.

Annual service

A fall tune-up costs less than an emergency visit and catches most of these problems early.

Boiler repair itself is maintenance and is not covered by homeowners insurance. Water damage from a burst heating pipe during a freeze may be covered if the home was being heated, so keep records of service.

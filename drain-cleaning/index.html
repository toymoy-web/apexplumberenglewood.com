Drain Cleaning & Unclogging in Englewood, CO

Drain cleaning is the removal of a blockage from a sink, tub, toilet, floor drain or main sewer line. The usual tools are a motorized cable, a camera to confirm what caused it, and water jetting when grease or roots keep coming back. Older Englewood drains: cast iron, clay and roots

A large share of Englewood houses were built in the decade after World War II. Drain systems from that era usually combine a cast iron stack inside the house with clay or cast iron sewer pipe running under the front yard to the district main.

Cast iron corrodes on the inside and builds up a rough scale that catches paper and grease, so older lines clog more easily than smooth plastic pipe of the same size. Clay pipe comes in short sections, and each joint is a place where fine tree roots can find moisture and grow in. The mature trees along many older Englewood streets make that a regular cause of main line backups.

A cable machine cuts roots out and restores flow the same day. It does not seal the joint, so roots return, often within a year. That is why a camera look after the line is clear matters. If the footage shows a cracked section or a joint that has shifted, the long-term fix is sewer line repair in Englewood rather than repeat cleaning.

Stop the backup from spreading while you wait

These steps are safe for any homeowner and can save a basement floor.

  • Stop the washing machine, dishwasher, showers and toilets everywhere in the house, including upstairs.
  • If sewage is on the floor, keep children and pets out of the area and avoid touching it without gloves.
  • Move boxes and rugs away from the floor drain area.
  • Do not pour bleach or drain chemicals into a backed-up line. They sit in the water and can burn skin during the repair.
  • Note whether the backup started during laundry, a shower or after heavy rain. It helps diagnose the cause.

How we clear a clog, and what we check afterward

The IPC (International Plumbing Code) calls for a cleanout at the base of each drain stack, and most older Englewood homes have one in the basement where the cast iron stack meets the floor. Some also have an exterior cleanout near the foundation. We open the cleanout closest to the blockage so the cable does not have to push through fixture traps.

Sink and tub lines get a small cable sized for 1.5 to 2 inch pipe. Main lines get a larger machine with cutting heads sized to the pipe. After flow is restored we run water for several minutes and, on main line calls, put a camera down the line so you can see what caused the clog.

When a line is coated in grease or packed with fine roots, cabling only punches a hole through the middle. Jetting, covered on our hydro jetting page, blasts the entire pipe wall clean and is the better choice for kitchen lines and for laterals that clog repeatedly.

Grease is a sewer problem you can prevent

South Englewood Sanitation District No. 1 asks residents to wipe fats and grease into the trash and warns that bleach poured down a drain can harden grease. South Platte Renew, the regional treatment plant, also runs an annual grease takeback program. Keeping grease out of the kitchen drain is the cheapest drain maintenance there is.

What affects the cost of drain cleaning

A single sink clog and a root-filled main line are very different jobs. These are the factors behind the quote.

Which line

Fixture drains are quicker than main lines, which need larger equipment and more setup.

Cleanout access

A reachable cleanout saves time. Pulling a toilet or going through a roof vent adds labor.

Camera inspection

Recommended on any main line clog and on repeat problems, so the cause is known, not guessed.

Jetting versus cabling

Jetting takes more equipment but often lasts much longer on grease and root problems.

After-hours timing

Night, weekend and holiday calls carry a higher rate than a scheduled daytime visit.

Homeowners insurance does not pay for drain cleaning itself. Damage from a sewage backup is often excluded unless a sewer backup rider was added to the policy, which is worth checking before you need it.
